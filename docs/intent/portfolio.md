# Statement of Intent: Personal Portfolio Website

- **Outcome:** A 2025+ ultra-modern, interactive portfolio website that positions Elham as a high-caliber Flutter & mobile engineer with rare design taste, fluid interaction craft, and deep curiosity for AI & bespoke tools.
- **User:** Technical founders and engineering leaders looking for standout mobile talent, as well as ambitious peers seeking inspiration.
- **Why now:** Establish a distinctive personal brand that sets Elham far apart from generic CS student profiles and cookie-cutter portfolios.
- **Success:** A visitor spends >60 seconds engaging with the interactive 3D device viewport and leaves convinced of elite UI/UX craft and engineering depth, prompting immediate outreach or connection.
- **Constraint:** Ultra-smooth performance (locked 60fps, fast initial load, responsive layout with graceful fallback/optimization on mobile devices) — 3D polish must never compromise speed or accessibility.
- **Out of scope:** An exploratory 3D game or virtual room, a traditional resume-style static document, or an exhaustive dump of basic coursework projects.
